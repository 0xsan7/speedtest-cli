# Speed Test Benchmarks

## Performance Standards

### Excellent Internet
- Download: 100+ Mbps
- Upload: 20+ Mbps
- Ping: 0-20 ms

### Good Internet
- Download: 50-100 Mbps
- Upload: 10-20 Mbps
- Ping: 20-50 ms

### Average Internet
- Download: 20-50 Mbps
- Upload: 5-10 Mbps
- Ping: 50-100 ms

### Poor Internet
- Download: Below 20 Mbps
- Upload: Below 5 Mbps
- Ping: Above 100 ms

## Use Cases by Speed

### 4K Video Streaming
- Required: 25+ Mbps download
- Recommended: 50+ Mbps

### HD Video Streaming
- Required: 5+ Mbps download
- Recommended: 10+ Mbps

### Online Gaming
- Required: 5+ Mbps download
- Critical: Low ping (under 50 ms)

### Video Conferencing
- Required: 2.5+ Mbps download/upload
- Recommended: 5+ Mbps

### Large File Downloads
- 50 Mbps = 80 MB in 13 seconds
- 100 Mbps = 80 MB in 6.4 seconds

## Testing Tips

1. Run multiple tests at different times
2. Close other applications during testing
3. Use wired connection for most accurate results
4. Check history to track speed trends
